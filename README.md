Entrants send exactly one emoji for their dream vacation and a judge picks the most evocative. Free to enter, one winner takes the whole reward.

## The entry

🗻 — Mount Fuji, Japan. One emoji, nothing else.

Bounty: [One Emoji, One Dream Vacation](https://make.fun/bounty/u-grand-elephant-19-one-emoji-one-dream-vacation-62879a35) — $2, winner-take-all, free to enter.

Why this one, against the judge's brief in its own order: it plainly names a dream vacation (the brief lists "a mountain" first among the examples — Fuji IS the mountain, and Japan IS the faraway place); it is a postcard at a glance (a snow-capped cone under a blue sky); it is one specific place and one particular dream, not any beach anywhere; it is the fresh pick in a field flooded with beach, palm, plane and sun; and its cone silhouette reads instantly at small size across cultures. One character, 4 bytes UTF-8 — the minimum size, which wins the byte tie-break.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes exactly one emoji to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). Deterministic — the same glyph on every run. Stdlib only; no network, no files read, no randomness.
